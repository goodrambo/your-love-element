# Product Blueprint

Last updated: 2026-05-13

## Product

`Your Love Element` 是一個英文市場的數位 relationship reading 產品。

核心承諾：

- 用戶完成免費 10 題 reading。
- 立即得到一份個人化 preview。
- 如果 preview 有價值，再付費解鎖完整 relationship report。
- 完整報告價格：`$9.99 USD`。
- 報告透過 email 交付。

產品定位：

- Five Element-inspired relationship reading。
- 個人反思與娛樂用途。
- 不是醫療、心理、法律、財務或專業建議。
- 語氣要 romantic、reflective、premium、calm。
- 避免保證式承諾，例如 guaranteed soulmate、exact date prediction。

## Funnel

```text
Ad / organic content
  -> Homepage
  -> 10-question free reading
  -> Personalized free preview
  -> Email entry
  -> Lemon Squeezy checkout
  -> /full-report/?reading_id=...
  -> 8 deeper paid signals
  -> Worker queues report generation
  -> OpenAI generates report
  -> Resend delivers report email
```

Free preview goals:

## Pricing

Current price:

```text
$9.99 USD
```

Reasoning:

- Low enough for impulse purchase after a free preview.
- High enough to justify personalized AI generation and email fulfillment.
- Leaves little margin for inefficient paid traffic, so CPA discipline matters.

Early paid acquisition target:

```text
Target CPA: $6-8
Learning ceiling: $10-12
```

For cold ads, early tests should be treated as funnel/message validation before scale.

## Product Boundaries

Always keep these boundaries visible in copy or policy pages:

- reflective personal insight
- entertainment
- optional full report
- no professional advice
- personalized digital product
- support available for delivery, duplicate charge, failed payment, or technical issue

Avoid:

- medical/mental-health claims
- exact future prediction
- guaranteed love outcome
- exploitative heartbreak language
- negative personal attributes in ads

## Reusable Product Pattern

This project proves a reusable product shape:

```text
Free diagnostic quiz
  -> useful preview
  -> low-ticket paid unlock
  -> paid follow-up questions
  -> AI-generated personalized report
  -> email delivery
  -> organic/share loop
```

This pattern can be reused for:

- love/relationship reports
- career archetype reports
- creator/business personality reports
- wellness reflection reports
- style/aesthetic profile reports
- compatibility or self-discovery products
