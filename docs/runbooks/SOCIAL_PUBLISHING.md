# Social Publishing

## Evergreen rules

- Public content is English unless the user explicitly requests another language.
- Keep claims reflective and non-deterministic: no guaranteed reunion, exact timing promise, fixed success rate, or assertion about a viewer's private relationship state.
- Do not expose internal dates, `Day` labels, post numbers, scene counters, or production notes inside creative output.
- Use a preview-first product promise: the 10-question preview is free; the full report is optional.
- Generated media belongs under `artifacts/` and must receive visual/media QA before publishing.
- A dated pack is not “current” after its schedule window. Confirm publication status and metrics before planning the next pack.
- Publishing, scheduling, ad creation, or spend requires explicit authorization.

Historical June 2026 packs and their copy, renderers, and QA sheets are preserved under `artifacts/archive/2026-07-12/assets/social/`.
